# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple calculator that calculates simple interest.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Where:
- Principal is the initial amount of money
- Rate is the annual interest rate
- Time is the period in years

## Example

If the principal is $1000, the interest rate is 5%, and the time is 2 years:

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100

Simple Interest = $100
